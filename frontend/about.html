Reviewer 2.0

Reviewer 2.0 reviews an academic paper the way a real program committee would — by splitting the work across a panel of independent reviewers, each focused on one dimension of the paper, then having a supervising editor read every reviewer's notes and summarize what matters most across all of them.

How a review works
  1. Upload a PDF (parsed into structured text via GROBID) or a pre-parsed JSON paper.
  2. Choose your panel — pick which of the rubrics below you want reviewed.
  3. Each selected rubric is handed to its own tool-using AI agent, which decides for itself which sections of the paper to read (and, for citations, checks them against the real literature via Semantic Scholar) before scoring the paper 1–10 with written justification.
  4. A supervising agent reads every reviewer's findings and writes a short synthesis, on top of a deterministic overall score and recommendation computed from the panel's scores.
